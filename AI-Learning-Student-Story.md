# AI Learning for Curious Kids: A 15-Year-Old, a 12-Year-Old, Mom, and Two Learning Websites

## Medium Post Draft

### Title
AI Basics for Students: How a 15-Year-Old Built a Learning Website and a 12-Year-Old Built a Fun AI-Powered Family Project

### Subtitle
A mom-led, AI-assisted journey that turned curiosity into real websites: one for AI fundamentals and one for interactive Indian cuisine.

### Intro
When I set out to help my kids learn AI, it became more than a homework exercise — it became a story our family could share.

My 15-year-old built a modern site called **AI Basics** at `https://ai-basics.org`, where students can explore AI concepts like neural networks, transformers, LLMs, and more. My 12-year-old built a second creative project with me: an interactive Indian cuisine learning site that blends recipes, culture, and playful AI support.

### Why this matters
This is not just a technology project. It is proof that young learners can build meaningful digital experiences when they are guided with encouragement, curiosity, and the right tools.

- Students don’t need PhDs to understand AI.
- They can build their own websites and teach others as they learn.
- AI can be a learning partner, not just a mysterious black box.

### What we built together

#### 1. AI Basics — `https://ai-basics.org`
This site is designed to help students understand AI in a friendly way.

- Clear, modern layout with a strong hero section and easy navigation.
- 20 AI concepts explained simply, no fluff.
- Glossary, quiz, and chapter-style learning.
- Friendly design that shows AI can be accessible and fun.

This project was led by my 15-year-old, who shaped the content, structure, and style to make AI feel less intimidating.

#### 2. Family AI Project — Indian cuisine learning site
The second website is a family-created experience from my 12-year-old and me, built with AI help.

- Interactive recipe and dish exploration.
- Hands-on ingredient drag-and-drop and recipe discovery.
- A celebration of Indian cuisine, culture, and storytelling.
- Designed to be visually warm, welcoming, and playful.

This project shows how AI can support creativity, not just technical learning.

### What students can learn from these sites

- How to organize ideas into a website.
- How to explain technical concepts clearly.
- How to use design and interactivity to make learning memorable.
- How AI can be part of a family learning experience.

If you are a parent or student wondering how to start with AI, here is the simplest takeaway:

- Start with curiosity.
- Build something real, even if it is small.
- Ask questions, and let AI help you shape the learning journey.

Our family’s advice: every student can learn AI by teaching it to others.
